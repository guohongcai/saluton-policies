Support — VoiceVault

VoiceVault App Store support · Last updated 2026-10-31

Getting started

  1. Install VoiceVault on iPhone.
  2. Open the Watch app on iPhone → Available Apps → install VoiceVault on Apple Watch.
  3. On Apple Watch, open VoiceVault and tap Start.
  4. Return to iPhone; open VoiceVault and wait for sync and transcription.

Permissions

VoiceVault asks for microphone access when you first record on Apple Watch. VoiceVault 1.0 does not request Health access and does not collect location. Recording, transcription and local search work with microphone access alone.

Recording stops

Calls, Siri, system alerts, low battery, storage limits and system interruptions can stop recording. Reopen VoiceVault on Watch and tap Start again. Check the recording indicator during important sessions. Audio that was never captured cannot be recovered.

Sync is waiting

Keep iPhone and Watch close, open the iPhone app, and tap Refresh. Large recordings may take time. If a transfer fails, keep the app open and try again; the app verifies each audio chunk before accepting it. If it still fails, export diagnostics from Settings.

Transcription quality

VoiceVault 1.0 transcribes Chinese, English, Japanese and Korean on device. Accuracy depends on microphone distance, noise and overlapping speech. The app does not identify individual speakers. Check important details against the original audio.

AI is not configured

AI is optional. Add your own API key in Settings → AI. Recording, transcription and search work without it. Provider charges are separate. Removing AI settings stops future requests but does not delete data already received by the provider.

Where is my data?

On your devices only. There is no VoiceVault server copy and the archive is excluded from iCloud and computer backups. Export important content separately. Removing the app removes the local archive.

Delete or export

You can delete a segment, an hour, a day, or all data in the app. You can export a day or an hour as audio, text, Markdown and JSON.

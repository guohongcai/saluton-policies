Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Will my data sync?

No cloud is used. All data is stored locally; a future version will offer manual export (JSON) / iCloud backup.

Why are the basic features locked?

They are unlocked through Pro payment. You can purchase a subscription or a one-time buyout inside the App, and cancel at any time. Prices are converted automatically according to your Apple Store country.

I can't find my Pro subscription?

Open Settings → Restore Purchases (for example, inside MigraineVault). Network issues are retried automatically.

The Widget shows nothing?

Open the main App for a second and the Widget will sync data automatically from the App Group; if it is still empty, check the App Group in system settings or verify signing in Xcode.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the App completely removes all local data.

Found a bug?

Email us: elk-sort7q@icloud.com, or leave a 5★ review on the App Store page so the team can respond faster.

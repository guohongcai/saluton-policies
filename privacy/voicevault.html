Privacy Policy — VoiceVault

VoiceVault App Store privacy policy · Last updated 2026-10-31

Last updated: 2026-10-30

VoiceVault is provided by GUO HANGJIANG ("we"). This policy explains how the app handles information.

Summary

  • We do not collect data.
  • No account is required.
  • Recording, transcription, search and storage happen on your devices.
  • AI is optional and uses a provider you choose with your own API key.

Data processed on your devices

  • Audio you record on Apple Watch.
  • Transcripts, search indexes, highlights, summaries, reflections and chat history.
  • Settings and preferences.
  • Optional body-context labels derived from Apple Health (heart rate, HRV, sleep, steps, workouts).
  • Optional approximate place names derived from location while recording.

This data is stored locally on your devices. We do not operate a server that receives your archive and cannot access it remotely.

Optional AI

If you configure a third-party AI provider, the text and context needed for the request are sent directly from your iPhone to that provider. Original audio is not uploaded. The provider's privacy and retention practices apply. You can remove the configuration at any time.

Optional cloud transcription

If you enable a cloud or self-hosted transcription service, audio is sent directly to the endpoint you configure after a separate consent notice. On-device transcription remains available without this.

Health and location

Health access is read-only. We do not write stress estimates to Apple Health. Location is used only while you record and is reduced to approximate place names. You can disable either feature in Settings.

Retention and deletion

You choose how long audio is kept. Deleting audio does not delete text unless you choose to. You can delete a segment, an hour, a day or all data in the app. The archive is excluded from iCloud and computer backups; removing the app removes the local archive.

Children

VoiceVault is not directed to children. Do not record other people without the notice and consent required by law.

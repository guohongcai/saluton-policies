Privacy Policy — DayVault

com.saluton.dayvault App Store privacy notice · Last updated 2026-09-29

1. Overview

DayVault (the name it is published under on the App Store) is designed, developed, and owned by us. We fully comply with Apple's App Store privacy rules (App Tracking Transparency & Privacy Nutrition Labels) and treat Data Not Collected as a baseline requirement of the product design.

This privacy policy applies to all available versions of DayVault (App Store releases and TestFlight builds). It explains how it handles your data when you install, use, or update DayVault, and what rights you have.

2. We collect no data (Data Not Collected)

When you open this App, it does not:

  • Upload or sync any of your content (there is no backend server)
  • Request location, contacts, photos, microphone, or photo library (except for explicit user actions)
  • Access Apple's IDFA advertising identifier, enable advertising SDKs, or perform ad tracking
  • Use third-party advertising / analytics / crash SDKs (nor any of the three categories above)
  • Track your device ID or activity

All data within the App (user input and subscription status) is stored only in the local App sandbox.

3. Local data you store yourself

Content you enter (vaccination/medication records, body temperature, countdown days, holiday calendar, passcode-lock settings) is stored on-device in SQLite / UserDefaults and is accessible only to this process.

iCloud: DayVault does not use iCloud sync. If a future version enables it, we will follow China's Personal Information Protection Law and the GDPR data-minimization principle, and will proactively ask for your consent.

4. Payments and subscriptions

Subscriptions / in-app purchases / one-time purchases in DayVault are all handled by Apple, with the App Store acting as the intermediary. We do not collect your payment information, Apple ID, device ID, address, or any personally identifiable information.

Subscription terms: payments are processed by Apple, and refunds are handled by Apple Support (getsupport.apple.com).

5. Children's privacy

DayVault is intended for ages 4+ and is not directed at children under 13; it does not collect any data.

6. Open source and compliance manifest

This App's privacy manifest template file can be reviewed online: github.com/saluton-apps (if it has not been uploaded, contact support to request it).

This App's PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy explicitly contains no "required reason APIs" that Apple deems disclosable — so compliance passes Apple's automated scan through the manifest bundled with the App, with no manual submission required.

7. Policy changes

If we change how we handle data (for example, by adding cloud sync or crash tracking in the future), we will update this page before the new version is released and will proactively ask for your consent.

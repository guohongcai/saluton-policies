Privacy Policy — MigraineVault

MigraineVault App Store privacy notice · Last updated 2026-11-06

1. Overview

MigraineVault is a private migraine and headache diary for adults. It is designed, developed, and owned by us. This policy explains what data the App uses, where it is stored, and what choices you have.

MigraineVault has no account system, no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking across apps or websites. Your migraine and health entries stay on your device.

2. Data stored on your device

The following information is stored locally in the App's sandbox (SwiftData / UserDefaults) and is accessible only to the App on your device:

  • Migraine attacks: date/time, severity, duration, pain location, symptoms, triggers and notes
  • Daily check-in: sleep, stress, hydration, caffeine, optional barometric pressure and period-started flag
  • Medications and dose logs
  • App settings: language, reminder times, Weather sync switch, Trigger-day reminder switch and App Lock
  • Widget snapshot data shared with the MigraineWidget through the App Group

We do not upload, sync or store your health entries on a developer server. MigraineVault does not use iCloud or CloudKit in this version. If you delete the App, the local data is deleted with it and cannot be recovered by us.

3. Location and weather / barometric pressure

Weather correlation is an optional feature. When it is enabled, MigraineVault requests When In Use location access once to fetch local air pressure and weather from Open-Meteo, a free public weather API.

  • Only coarse location is used: coordinates are rounded to 0.1° (about 50 km) before the request.
  • The rounded point is cached locally for up to 7 days so the App can reuse it without asking again.
  • No name, email, account ID, advertising identifier or device identifier is sent with the request.
  • Open-Meteo returns pressure/weather data; we do not store your location on a developer server.
  • You can turn off Weather sync in Settings at any time. If you deny location access, the rest of the App still works; only the weather/pressure section is unavailable.

International processing: weather requests are sent to Open-Meteo, which may process the request outside your country or region. The request contains only rounded coordinates and no user identity. If you do not want this, keep Weather sync off.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes payment and billing; we do not receive your payment card details, Apple ID password or billing address.

We use RevenueCat to validate subscription receipts and determine whether Pro features are unlocked. RevenueCat processes purchase history and subscription status for this purpose. It is not used for advertising, tracking or building a profile of you. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. Refunds are handled by Apple Support.

5. Permissions we request

  • Location (When In Use): optional, only for the weather/barometric pressure feature described above.
  • Notifications: optional, for medication reminders, daily check-in reminders and trigger-day alerts. Notifications are scheduled locally on your device.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: optional, used only by the App Lock feature through Apple's LocalAuthentication. Biometric data is handled by iOS and is never accessible to us.

You can change these permissions in iOS Settings. Denying an optional permission does not prevent you from using the core diary.

6. Third-party services

  • Apple App Store / StoreKit: payment processing, subscription management and receipt validation.
  • RevenueCat: subscription entitlement validation. See RevenueCat privacy policy.
  • Open-Meteo: weather and barometric pressure data. See Open-Meteo terms.

We do not use advertising networks, analytics SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, social login, AI services or data brokers.

7. App Privacy label and privacy manifest

Our App Store privacy label declares:

  • Coarse Location — App Functionality; not linked to your identity; not used for tracking.
  • Purchase History — App Functionality; not linked to your identity; not used for tracking.

All other data types are not collected. The App's PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy declares no tracking, no tracking domains, the required-reason UserDefaults API and the same collected data types. We do not access the advertising identifier (IDFA) or ask for App Tracking Transparency permission.

8. Children's privacy

MigraineVault is a self-management tool intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help you delete the local data.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Disable Weather sync to stop location-based weather requests.
  • Disable notifications or App Lock in Settings or iOS Settings.
  • Delete individual entries in the App, or delete the App to remove all local data.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions through Apple.
  • Contact us with privacy questions or requests.

Because health entries are stored only on your device, we cannot access, export or delete them for you; you control them directly in the App.

10. Security and retention

Health entries remain in the App's local sandbox and are protected by iOS app sandboxing. Optional App Lock adds Face ID / Touch ID / passcode protection. We retain no server-side copy of your entries. Weather location cache is kept for up to 7 days and can be removed by deleting the App.

11. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds a new data practice (for example cloud sync or crash reporting), we will update this page before release and update the App Store privacy label and privacy manifest. The “Last updated” date at the top will change.
